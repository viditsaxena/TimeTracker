# TimeTracker 1.1.0

You can now fix a session you tracked incorrectly. Open the TimeTracker window and click the session in the list. Change its start time, duration, or category, then click **Save**. The daily and weekly totals update right away.

You can type `45` for 45 minutes, `1:30` for an hour and a half, or `0:45:30` if you need seconds. If the new time overlaps another session, TimeTracker will ask you to change it. Your other sessions are left alone.

Download **TimeTracker-v1.1.0-macOS-universal.zip** below, unzip it, and move **TimeTracker.app** to **Applications**. Quit the old app before replacing it. Your saved sessions stay on your Mac.

You need macOS 14 or newer. The ZIP includes Apple Silicon and Intel versions. I've tested it on Apple Silicon, but not on an Intel Mac yet. Apple hasn't verified the app, so your Mac may stop you the first time you open it. If you trust the download, try opening it once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. [Apple explains this here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

The **SHA256SUMS.txt** file below has a checksum for the download. [The README](https://github.com/viditsaxena/TimeTracker) has the full instructions.
