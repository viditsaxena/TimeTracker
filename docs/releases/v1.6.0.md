# TimeTracker 1.6.0

Starting a timer now opens a small Work or Music popup. Click the one you're doing and it goes away. If you close the popup without choosing, the session stays Work. Choosing Music marks the whole new session as Music; it doesn't leave a tiny Work session behind.

You can also delete a session now. Click the trash icon beside it, then confirm Delete on the same row. The totals and bars update when it's gone.

This version includes the single Add control near the top and the Option–T shortcut from the previous update. If you still see the old buttons beside every session, quit TimeTracker and open the new app from Applications. Your saved sessions are kept separately from the app.

Download **TimeTracker-v1.6.0-macOS-universal.zip** below, unzip it, and move **TimeTracker.app** to **Applications**. Quit the old app before replacing it. Your saved sessions will still be there.

You need macOS 14 or newer. The ZIP includes Apple Silicon and Intel versions. I've tested it on Apple Silicon, but not on an Intel Mac yet. Apple hasn't verified the app, so your Mac may stop you the first time you open it. If you trust the download, try opening it once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. [Apple explains this here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

The **SHA256SUMS.txt** file below has a checksum for the download. [The README](https://github.com/viditsaxena/TimeTracker) has the full instructions.
