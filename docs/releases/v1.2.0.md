# TimeTracker 1.2.0

You can now add time you forgot to track. Maybe you were on a call away from your computer, or you just forgot to start the timer.

Click **Add missed time** in the TimeTracker window or menu bar. Choose when the work ended and whether it was Work or Music. Then click **5 min**, **10 min**, **15 min**, or **20 min**. That's it—the new session is saved. For a longer block, type a duration like `45` minutes or `1:30` and click **Add**.

The daily and weekly totals update as soon as you add it. If the time overlaps another session, TimeTracker will ask you to choose a different time or length.

Download **TimeTracker-v1.2.0-macOS-universal.zip** below, unzip it, and move **TimeTracker.app** to **Applications**. Quit the old app before replacing it. Your saved sessions will still be there.

You need macOS 14 or newer. The ZIP includes Apple Silicon and Intel versions. I've tested it on Apple Silicon, but not on an Intel Mac yet. Apple hasn't verified the app, so your Mac may stop you the first time you open it. If you trust the download, try opening it once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. [Apple explains this here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

The **SHA256SUMS.txt** file below has a checksum for the download. [The README](https://github.com/viditsaxena/TimeTracker) has the full instructions.
