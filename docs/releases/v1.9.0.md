# TimeTracker 1.9.0

Press **Option–T** and Work starts right away. There's no project popup or Enter key to press. Press **Option–T** again to stop. To start Music directly, click TimeTracker in the menu bar and choose **Music**.

If you start Work and switch to Music within 10 seconds, that tiny Work block isn't saved. New Work, Music, and standing blocks shorter than 5 seconds aren't saved either. Your old sessions are left alone.

Download **TimeTracker-v1.9.0-macOS-universal.zip** below, unzip it, and move **TimeTracker.app** to **Applications**. Quit the old app before replacing it. Your saved sessions will still be there.

You need macOS 14 or newer. The ZIP includes Apple Silicon and Intel versions. I've tested it on Apple Silicon, but not on an Intel Mac yet. Apple hasn't verified the app, so your Mac may stop you the first time you open it. If you trust the download, try opening it once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. [Apple explains this here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

The **SHA256SUMS.txt** file below has a checksum for the download. [The README](https://github.com/viditsaxena/TimeTracker) has the full instructions.
